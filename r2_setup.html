Six steps — then paste me one link

Do these on your Mac, logged into Cloudflare. Each step says what to click, what to type, and what you should see next.
You click, I code. I can't take your password or sign in for you. When you reach step 6, paste the link into the chat and I do the rest.
Steps
  1. Open R2 (this is the tricky one)
    • go to dash.cloudflare.com and log in.
    • you land on the account home.
    • in the left sidebar, under the Build heading, click Storage & databases.
    • that opens the storage products — click R2 Object Storage (or just R2).
    • can't find it? use this direct link, which jumps straight there: dash.cloudflare.com/…/r2/overview
    • first time only: it shows Purchase R2 and asks for a credit card. Add one — the free tier (10 GB) is $0; the card just switches R2 on.
    • done when you see the R2 Overview page with a blue Create bucket button (upper right).
  2. Create the bucket
    • click Create bucket.
    • Bucket name: type vampjam-audio (all lowercase — that exact name).
    • leave Location and everything else default. Click Create bucket.
    • you're now inside the bucket, on its Objects tab (empty).
  3. Upload the recording
    • click the Upload button (top right) → Select files.
    • choose Sound.Union.2026-08-07.v2.m4a — it's in your vampjam folder (or on your Desktop).
    • a progress bar runs; wait until it says done. ~137 MB, a minute or two.
  4. Open the bucket's Settings
    • at the top of the bucket page, click the Settings tab.
    • scroll to the Public Development URL section.
  5. Turn on public access
    • under Public Development URL, click Enable.
    • a box asks "Allow Public Access?" — type allow and click Allow.
    • the setting now shows Public URL Access: Allowed, and a Public Bucket URL appears, like https://pub-abc123....r2.dev.
  6. Copy the link and paste it to me
    • copy that Public Bucket URL (the https://pub-….r2.dev part).
    • paste it into the chat. That's all I need — I'll add the filename and wire it in.
    • the finished audio link will be that URL + /Sound.Union.2026-08-07.v2.m4a.
After you paste the link: I point the Aug 7 page at it, push, and you hard-reload the session on your iPhone and tap play. If it plays, every future recording just gets dropped into this same vampjam-audio bucket — no more GitHub release-asset failures.
