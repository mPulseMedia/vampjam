Make the worker

a "worker" is a small program of yours that lives on cloudflare — the same place your audio and your session list already live. this one checks whether a phone number is on a list. that is the whole of its job now. you already have vampjam-upload, vampjam-sync and page-agent-keeper there; this is another of the same kind.

  1. dash.cloudflare.com, sign in. Left menu: Compute → Workers & Pages. Your own workers listed, and a blue Create application right of the heading. drawn from cloudflare's own screen, not yours
  2. Click Create application. A box comes up headed Make something new. Take Start with Hello World! Not GitHub, not GitLab, not a template, not upload — those all want a repository or files you do not have. Ignore Continue to Pages at the bottom; that is the old workflow, and it is for websites rather than workers. drawn from cloudflare's own screen, not yours
  3. Name it vampjam-auth — exactly that, lower case, with the hyphen. Then Deploy. Says the name is taken? You already made it. Open it from the list and carry on at B.
B

Paste the real program in

the button below hands you the program. this page carries it, and the test suite checks it against what is committed, so what you paste is what is meant to be there. nothing in it needs editing.

  1. Tap the dark line below — it copies itself. About 200 lines. The label tells you how big it was. Nothing happens, or it says it could not copy? Tap show it under the button and select the text by hand, or open the file on GitHub and use ⌘A ⌘C. All three give you exactly the same thing.
  2. On vampjam-auth: Edit code, top right. Click inside the code, ⌘A to select all, ⌘V to paste over it. Then Deploy. Done when the button settles and there is no red anywhere.
  3. Near the top of the worker page is an address ending .workers.dev. The site expects vampjam-auth.crimson-dust-a18d.workers.dev If yours is different, paste it to me — one line for me to change. Do not rename anything.

Give people a recording

    
this is the part you will actually use, and it is not on this page — it is at the
    bottom of each recording. open the one you want to share and scroll to the end of it.

  

  
    
    
  1. 
      Open any recording. At the very bottom, a box headed Who can open this recording.
      Cannot see it? Only the administrator can — and nobody is the
      administrator until a first number is added. The very first number added anywhere becomes
      it, so make the first one your own.
    
    2. 
    
  2. 
      Paste phone numbers into the box. One per line, or separated by commas — however they
      came out of Messages or Contacts. Names on the same line are fine:
      Dave 415 555 1212. Then Add them.
      Each becomes a small tag with the name and the last two digits. Anything
      that was not a phone number is listed back to you rather than quietly dropped.
      Adding the first person makes that recording private. Everything you
      do not touch stays open to anyone with the link, exactly as it is today.
    
    3. 
    
  3. 
      They go to the sign-in page, type that number, and they are in. No
      text message, no link, no waiting.
      A number added to one recording opens that recording. Add the same number
      at the bottom of two and they get two.
      Taking someone off: the × on their tag. It takes effect on
      their next tap.
    
    4. 
    
  4. 
      Open that recording in a private browser window. You should get the sign-in panel instead of
      the player and the moments.
      Changed your mind? Let anyone in, in the same box, puts it back the
      way it was.

What this is not

    
a phone number is not a password. anyone who knows a number you have added can type it and
    be signed in as that person. that is the trade you made when you dropped the text message, and
    it is a fair one for keeping a jam session out of the way of strangers and search engines. it
    is not a fair one for anything you would mind being wrong about.

    
and the audio files still sit at a public address. someone who already has that exact
    address can still play the file, gate or no gate. the lock is a separate job: the audio moves
    behind the worker and the public address is switched off. say the word and I will do it. it
    changes how all nine existing recordings load, so it wants its own build.
