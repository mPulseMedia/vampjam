A

Three things from Twilio

twilio is the company that actually sends the text message. you need three things off their site: an account SID, an auth token, and a phone number to send from. nothing here touches vampsf yet.

  1. Go to console.twilio.com and sign in. If you have never used it, sign up and take the free trial. You should land on a page headed Account Info. A trial can only text numbers you have verified. Fine for testing on your own phone; you will need a card on file before it can text anyone else.
  2. On that page find Account SID — a long string starting AC. Copy it and paste it into Notes for a minute. drawn from twilio's own screen, not yours
  3. Just below it, Auth Token — click Show to reveal, then copy. This one is a password. It goes into one box on Cloudflare in step D3 and nowhere else. Do not paste it into a chat, including to me. drawn from twilio's own screen, not yours
  4. Left menu: Phone Numbers → Manage → Active numbers. Copy the number, in the form +14155551212 — keep the +1. If the list is empty: Buy a number, tick SMS in the capabilities filter, search, buy. About $1.15 a month plus roughly a cent per text. drawn from twilio's own screen, not yours
B

Make the worker

a "worker" is a small program of yours that lives on cloudflare — the same place your audio and your session list already live. this one checks who is on your list and asks twilio to send the text. you already have two workers there; this is a third.

  1. dash.cloudflare.com, sign in. Left menu: Compute (Workers), or Workers & Pages on the older layout. You should see vampjam-upload and vampjam-sync already listed. Those are yours.
  2. Create → Workers → Create Worker (or the Hello World starter). Name it vampjam-auth — exactly that, the site looks for that name. Then Deploy. It deploys a do-nothing program. That is expected; the real one goes in next. drawn from cloudflare's own screen, not yours
C

Paste the real program in

the button below hands you the exact program that is committed to your repo — it is fetched off the site when you tap it, so there is no second copy anywhere to drift out of date. nothing in it needs editing.

  1. Tap the dark line below — it copies itself. About 200 lines.
  2. On vampjam-auth: Edit code, top right (sometimes behind a </> icon). Click inside the code, ⌘A to select all, ⌘V to paste over it. Then Deploy. Done when the button settles and there is no red anywhere. drawn from cloudflare's own screen, not yours
  3. Near the top of the worker page is an address ending .workers.dev. The site expects vampjam-auth.crimson-dust-a18d.workers.dev If yours is different, paste it to me — one line for me to change. Do not rename anything.
D

Five settings

same worker: Settings → Variables and Secrets → Add, five times. the type matters — the first three are Secret, the last two are Text.

  1. AUTH_SECRET · type Secret · a long random string you invent. nothing depends on what it is; changing it later signs everybody out, which is your emergency lever. Tap the dark line below and it invents one and copies it. A different one every tap. drawn from cloudflare's own screen, not yours
  2. TWILIO_SID · type Secret · the Account SID from A2, starting AC.
  3. TWILIO_TOKEN · type Secret · the Auth Token from A3.
  4. TWILIO_FROM · type Text · your Twilio number from A4, with the plus: +14155551212.
  5. SITE · type Text · https://vampsf.com — then Deploy once more. Settings only take effect on a deploy. Skipping this is the most common reason it still looks broken afterwards.

Check it took

come back here and press check again at the top. the first three lights should fill. the last three are about people and recordings — those are F and G.

  1. Scroll to the top of this page and press check again. If it still says the worker is not there: the name is probably not exactly vampjam-auth, or the address in C3 is different. Send me the address.
F

Put people on the list

phone or Mac, either is fine. no phone number is ever stored on the site: the worker scrambles each one with its own secret, and the list keeps the scrambled version, the name you type, and the last four digits.

  1. Open Admin → Who can sign in. Type your name and your own mobile number → Add. The first number added becomes the administrator. It has to be yours.
  2. Prove it on yourself: Sign in → your number → Text me a link → tap the link in the text. You land back signed in, and the Sign in row at the foot of the session list now shows your name. If no text arrives, the page prints what Twilio said. A trial account refuses any number you have not verified — verify yours under Verified Caller IDs, or add a card.
  3. Add the others, one at a time. Nobody gets a text now — only when they ask to sign in.
G

Close a recording

anything you do not touch stays open to anyone with the link, exactly as it is today.

  1. Admin → Private recordings → find the one → make private → tick who may open it. Unticking takes it away on their next tap. No waiting.
  2. Open that recording in a private browser window. You should get the sign-in panel instead of the player and the moments.
!

What this is not

the audio files still sit at a public address. someone who already has that exact address can still play the file directly. this closes the door on the site — it is not a locked door.

the lock is a separate job: the audio moves behind the worker and the public address is switched off. say the word and I will do it. it changes how all nine existing recordings load, so it wants its own build rather than being smuggled into this one.
