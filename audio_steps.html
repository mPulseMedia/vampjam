Put a recording up

Five steps. Four are yours, because I cannot reach the bucket from here. The fifth is mine and takes a minute.
Use this for a file you already have — something you recorded elsewhere and want on the site. If you recorded it in vampSF on your phone, you do not need any of this: that upload already happened when you hit stop.

Nothing breaks while you do this. A file sitting in the bucket is not on the site yet. It only appears once I make its page, which is step 5.
Steps
  1. Name the file before you send it
    • The filename becomes part of the address, so it is worth thirty seconds.
    • Date first, then the place. Lowercase, underscores, no spaces:
    • 2026_09_08_sound_union.m4a
    • In Finder, click the file once, press return, and type the new name.
    • .m4a or .mp3 both play. Keep whatever extension it already has.
    • Leave this Finder window open — you drag out of it in step 4.
  2. Sign in to Cloudflare
    • Go to dash.cloudflare.com.
    • If it asks you to sign in, use the same account the bucket is on.
    • If a code is texted or emailed to you, that is the two-factor step — enter it.
    • You land on the account home, not on R2. That is expected; the next step gets you there.
  3. Open the bucket
    • In the left sidebar, click Storage & databases. It sits under the Build heading.
    • Click R2 Object Storage in the list that opens under it.
    • Click the bucket named vampjam-audio.
    • You land on its Objects tab, listing the files already up there.
    Drawn from Cloudflare's own documentation, not from your screen. If it does not look like this, send me a screenshot and I will redraw it.
  4. Drag the file onto the Objects list
    • Put the Finder window from step 1 beside the browser.
    • Drag the file onto the middle of the Objects list and let go.
    • An upload panel counts it off. Wait until it says it is done.
    • If dragging does not take, click Upload at the top right of the list and choose the file.
    • A big file is a sit-down, not a coffee — roughly a minute per 50 MB on home wifi.
    • When it is finished, the file appears as a row in the list. That row is how you know.
  5. Tell me the filename
    • Paste me the name exactly as it appears in the Objects list.
    • I make the recording's page, put it in the list, and send you the link.
    • It arrives open to anyone with the link. Add numbers at the bottom of it to make it private.
If something else happens
No bucket called vampjam-audio. Then it is under a different account. Sign out and back in with the other one, or send me a screenshot of what you do see.

The upload stalls or fails part way. Delete the half-finished row and drag it again — R2 does not resume, it restarts.

Anything the page did not predict. Send me the sentence on your screen.
