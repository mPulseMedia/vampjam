Paste the new upload worker into Cloudflare
Three minutes, on the Mac. Your 2h41m Redwood City recording is safe on your phone and is blocked only by this. The moment the worker is updated, the Record page sends it up by itself in 8 MB pieces — nothing else for you to do.
What was wrong. A 2h41m take is about 150 MB. Cloudflare stops any single upload at 100 MB, and the little program that receives your files (the "worker") also tried to hold the whole file in memory at once. Two walls, same place. Every try — yours and the page's own — got as far as registering the session and then hit that wall, which is why the session list shows it as syncing….

What changed. The Record page now sends a recording in 8 MB pieces and the worker stitches them back into one file. That needs the new worker code to be in Cloudflare, and Cloudflare is your login, not mine. That paste is the one thing waiting on you.

Until you do it the page will not keep banging on the wall: for a file this size it says plainly that the worker needs updating and sends you here. Short recordings still upload the old way in the meantime.
Steps — on the Mac
  1. Copy the new worker code
    • Tap the button. It copies the whole program — about 130 lines — nothing to edit.
    • cloudflare/r2_upload_worker.js in the repo, if you would rather open it there
  2. Open the worker in Cloudflare
    • Go to dash.cloudflare.com and sign in.
    • Left side: Workers & Pages. You should see a list with vampjam-upload and vampjam-sync in it.
    • Click vampjam-upload — that one, not sync.
    • Top right of that page: a button that says Edit code (on some screens it is under a </> icon). Click it. You are looking at a code editor with the old program in it.
  3. Replace what is there
    • Click anywhere inside the code, then ⌘A (select all), then ⌘V (paste). The old program is gone and the new one is in its place.
    • Top right: Deploy. Click it. If it asks to confirm, confirm.
    • You are done when the button goes quiet and there is no red anywhere. Nothing else on that page needs touching — the bucket and the settings are unchanged.
  4. Then just open the Record page on your phone
    • It finds the recording, says uploading 8.0 MB of 150 MB… and counts up. A couple of minutes on wifi.
    • When it is done it takes you straight to the session. If anything is off, tap send debug info to Claude at the bottom and tell me.

Optional — only if you want the second listen to be free
The light file is the whole win here: about a third of the bytes, every time. What it does not do is stop your phone fetching the file again the next time you open the session.

I cannot fix that from here, and I want to be straight about why. The address your files sit at (pub-…​.r2.dev) is Cloudflare's plain development address. It sends no caching instruction to your phone, and there is no setting on that address to change it. To change it you have to put a custom domain on the bucket — say audio.vampsf.com — and then add one Cache Rule that tells browsers to keep the file for a month. Roughly fifteen minutes in the Cloudflare dashboard.

Worth knowing before you spend those fifteen minutes: iPhone Safari throws audio out of its cache fairly aggressively on its own, so this buys less on a phone than it sounds like it should. It is a real improvement, not a large one.
