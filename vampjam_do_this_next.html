Steps
  1. Open your Mac's folder with the six files in it
    • In Finder, press command shift G. A small box opens at the top of the window.
    • Paste the path below into it and press return.
    • Six files appear. Leave this window open — you'll drag out of it in step 4.
    ~/claude_cowork/vampjam/audio/thin
  2. Sign in to Cloudflare
    • Go to dash.cloudflare.com.
    • If it asks you to sign in, use the same account the bucket is on.
    • If a code is texted or emailed to you, that's the two-factor step — enter it.
    • You land on the account home, not on R2. That's expected; the next step gets you there.
  3. Open the bucket
    • In the left sidebar, click Storage & databases. It sits under the Build heading.
    • Click R2 Object Storage in the list that opens under it.
    • Your bucket appears as a row in the middle of the page. Click its name.
    • You land on the bucket's Objects tab, listing the files already up there.
    Drawn from Cloudflare's own documentation, not from your screen — I haven't seen your dashboard. If it doesn't look like this, send me a screenshot and I'll redraw it.
  4. Drag the six files onto the Objects list
    • Put the Finder window from step 1 beside the browser.
    • Select all six files: click the first, then shift click the last.
    • Drag them onto the middle of the Objects list and let go.
    • An upload panel counts them off. Wait until all six say they're done.
    • If dragging doesn't take, click the Upload button at the top right of the list and choose the files instead.
    Drawn from Cloudflare's own documentation, not from your screen.
